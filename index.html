// OUR STORY

Born in the Underground

D Threads started as a one-person studio in a basement surrounded by soldering irons and sewing machines. We believe fashion should be a statement — not a uniform. Every piece we release is designed to disrupt, to glow, and to last.

We source ethically, manufacture locally, and ship globally. No fast fashion. No corner cutting. Just raw, unapologetic style built for the next generation.
