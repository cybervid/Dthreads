Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with D THREADS. This policy covers how we process, ship, and deliver your orders.

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

01

Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays) after payment confirmation.

  • You will receive an order confirmation email immediately after placing your order.
  • Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number.
  • During peak seasons, sales events, or holidays, processing times may be slightly extended — we will notify you if significant delays occur.

02

Shipping Methods & Carriers

We partner with trusted carriers such as DHL, FedEx, and UPS to ensure reliable and timely delivery.

At checkout, you may choose from the following options:

  • Standard Shipping
  • Express / Expedited Shipping
  • International Priority Shipping (where available)

We do not ship to P.O. Boxes, APO/FPO addresses, or restricted regions unless explicitly stated at checkout.

03

Shipping Costs

Shipping fees are calculated at checkout based on package weight and dimensions, destination, and the shipping method you select.

Free shipping promotions, when available, will be clearly advertised on our website and at checkout.

04

Estimated Delivery Times

Delivery times are estimates only, counted from the shipping date (not the order date).

Actual delivery may vary due to customs clearance, carrier delays, weather conditions, or other factors beyond our control. We do not guarantee delivery dates and are not liable for delays caused by third parties.

05

International Shipping — Customs, Duties & Taxes

For international orders, please be aware of the following:

  • Your destination country may impose customs duties, import taxes, VAT, or other fees.
  • These charges are not included in the product price or shipping cost and are the sole responsibility of the buyer.
  • The amount is determined by local customs authorities and must be paid by you upon delivery (or before release).
  • If you refuse to pay customs fees and the package is returned, abandoned, or destroyed, D THREADS is not responsible and the order amount (including shipping) will not be refunded.

We recommend contacting your local customs office for more information before placing an international order.

06

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipment confirmation email containing:

  • A unique tracking number
  • A link to track your package in real time

Please allow 24–72 hours for tracking information to update after dispatch.

07

Incorrect Address & Unclaimed Packages

You are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address.

If your order is returned to us due to an incorrect or incomplete address, an unclaimed package, or refusal of customs fees, we will:

  • Contact you to confirm the correct address
  • Charge you for re-shipping costs before we send it out again
  • If you choose not to re-ship, refund the product amount minus the original shipping costs

If you need to change your shipping address, please contact us within 24 hours of placing your order. Once dispatched, no changes or cancellations can be made.

08

Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Packages

8.1 Damage During Transit

If your package arrives visibly damaged:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Provide clear photos of the outer packaging and the damaged item(s).
  • After review, we will arrange a replacement or refund (at our discretion).

8.2 Lost or Stolen Packages

If the carrier marks your package as “Delivered” but you have not received it, responsibility shifts to you.

  • Please contact the carrier and your local post office immediately to file a claim.
  • We do not guarantee refunds for packages marked “Delivered” that are later reported lost or stolen.
  • For high-value orders, contact us before purchase to arrange additional shipping insurance (at an extra cost).

09

Delays & Force Majeure

D THREADS is not liable for shipping delays caused by:

  • Customs clearance backlogs
  • Carrier strikes, operational issues, or system failures
  • Severe weather or natural disasters
  • Political unrest, war, or other force majeure events

We will do our best to communicate any known delays and work with carriers to resolve issues promptly.
