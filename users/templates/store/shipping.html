Shipping Policy

01

Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays) after payment confirmation.

  • You will receive an order confirmation email immediately after placing your order.
  • Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number.
  • During peak seasons, sales events, or holidays, processing times may be slightly extended.

02

Shipping Methods & Carriers

We partner with trusted carriers such as DHL, FedEx, and UPS to ensure reliable and timely delivery.

At checkout, you may choose from the following options:

  • Standard Shipping
  • Express / Expedited Shipping
  • International Priority Shipping (where available)

We do not ship to P.O. Boxes, APO/FPO addresses, or restricted regions unless explicitly stated at checkout.

05

International Shipping — Customs, Duties & Taxes

For international orders, please be aware of the following:

  • Your destination country may impose customs duties, import taxes, VAT, or other fees.
  • These charges are the sole responsibility of the buyer.
  • If you refuse to pay customs fees and the package is returned, D THREADS is not responsible and the order will not be refunded.

06

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipment confirmation email containing a unique tracking number and a link to track your package in real time.

Please allow 24–72 hours for tracking information to update after dispatch.

07

Incorrect Address & Unclaimed Packages

You are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address. If your order is returned to us due to an incorrect or incomplete address, we will contact you to confirm the correct address and charge re-shipping costs.

If you need to change your shipping address, please contact us within 24 hours of placing your order.

08

Lost, Stolen, or Damaged Packages

8.1 Damage During Transit

If your package arrives visibly damaged, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the outer packaging and damaged item(s).

8.2 Lost or Stolen Packages

If the carrier marks your package as "Delivered" but you have not received it, please contact the carrier and your local post office immediately to file a claim.

09

Delays & Force Majeure

D THREADS is not liable for shipping delays caused by customs clearance, carrier strikes, severe weather, natural disasters, or other force majeure events.
